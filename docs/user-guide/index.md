# HyperTaxonomy User Guide

For WordPress site owners and content editors who configure and use the plugin. To install it and see a first result, start with [Getting Started](../getting-started.html).

This guide is split into the following pages:

- **[Settings Reference](settings-reference.html)** — every tab at Settings > HyperTaxonomy.
- **[Roles & Permissions](roles-permissions.html)** — the six custom capabilities and which roles get them.
- **[Import & Migration](import-migration.html)** — JSON export/import and migrating from Tag Groups (now PublishPress Tag Groups).
- **[Concepts](concepts.html)** — what terms and taxonomies are, the problem HyperTaxonomy solves, relationship types, Term Groups, and choosing between features.
- **[Term Relationships](term-relationships.html)** — Term Groups, inclusions, containers, synonyms, group inclusions, exclusions, and redirects.
- **[Per-Term Settings](per-term-settings.html)** — visibility, icons, archive descriptions, sticky posts, and archive overrides.
- **[Merge & Convert Terms](merge-convert.html)** — consolidating duplicate terms and moving terms between taxonomies.
- **[Term Lists & Clouds](term-lists.html)** — `[ht_term_list]`, `[ht_term_index]`, `[ht_term_cloud]`, `[ht_term_groups]`, and their blocks.
- **[Post Lists](post-lists.html)** — `[ht_post_list]`, `[ht_related_posts]`, and their blocks.
- **[Archive & Results Controls](archive-controls.html)** — sort order, filter panels, and the Gutenberg block extensions.
- **[Search Widget](search-widget.html)** — the advanced search widget, its fields and controllers, the shared results container, and POST mode results pages.
- **[PowerSearch](powersearch.html)** — the PowerSearch form and block, native search override, and a comparison of search methods.
- **[Editing Posts](editing-posts.html)** — the Tags field, inclusion and container badges, redirect indicators, hidden terms, and sticky pins in the post editor.
- **[Modeling Guide](modeling-guide.html)** — how to structure content with HyperTaxonomy: patterns, design principles, and example scenarios.
- **[Working with Other Plugins](other-plugins.html)** — how HyperTaxonomy fits alongside ACF, Pods, TaxoPress, FacetWP, SEO and translation plugins, and others.
- **[Deactivation & Uninstall](deactivation-uninstall.html)** — what stops working while the plugin is deactivated, what reactivation restores, and what uninstalling deletes.
- **[FAQ & Troubleshooting](faq.html)** — common questions and troubleshooting.
