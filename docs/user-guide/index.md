# HyperTaxonomy User Guide

For WordPress site owners and content editors who configure and use the plugin. To install it and see a first result, start with [Getting started](../#getting-started).

In wp-admin, HyperTaxonomy works in three places:

- **The Terms menu** in the admin sidebar — **[Term Groups](term-groups.html)**, **[Convert Terms](merge-convert.html#convert-terms)**, **[Merge Terms](merge-convert.html#merge-terms)**, **[Synonyms](term-relationships.html#the-synonyms-page)**, and **[Term Relationship Types](term-relationships.html#custom-relationship-types)**. Companion plugins add their own screens to it: **[Peer Sites](https://hypertaxonomy.org/termconnect/user-guide/peer-sites-feeds.html)** when [TermConnect](https://hypertaxonomy.org/termconnect/) is active, and **[Term Locations](https://hypertaxonomy.org/as-the-crow-flies/user-guide/locations-settings.html#for-all-terms)** when [As the Crow Flies](https://hypertaxonomy.org/as-the-crow-flies/) is active.
- **Term edit screens** — any tag, category or enabled custom taxonomy term: its groups, [relationships](term-relationships.html) and [per-term settings](per-term-settings.html). The term lists gain [columns, filters and bulk actions](per-term-settings.html#term-list-columns).
- **[Settings&nbsp;→ HyperTaxonomy](plugin-settings.html)** — which taxonomies and features are enabled, display, icons, search, cache, export/import and advanced options.

This guide is split into the following pages:

- **[Concepts](concepts.html)** — what terms and taxonomies are, Term Groups, relationship types, and which to use when.
- **[Term Groups](term-groups.html)** — creating groups, group archives, and editing a group: its members, the terms it contains or is included by, its icon, visibility and description.
- **[Term Relationships](term-relationships.html)** — inclusions and containers, group inclusions, container groups, synonyms and the Synonyms page, exclusions, redirects, custom relationship types, and reading the Term Relationships section.
- **[Per-Term Settings](per-term-settings.html)** — the term list columns, the term edit screen, group membership, visibility, icons, archive descriptions, sticky posts, and archive overrides.
- **[Plugin Settings](plugin-settings.html)** — every tab at Settings&nbsp;→ HyperTaxonomy.
- **[Merge & Convert Terms](merge-convert.html)** — consolidating duplicate terms and moving terms between taxonomies.
- **[Term Lists & Clouds](term-lists.html)** — `[ht_term_list]`, `[ht_term_index]`, `[ht_term_cloud]`, `[ht_term_groups]`, and their blocks.
- **[Post Lists](post-lists.html)** — `[ht_post_list]`, `[ht_related_posts]`, and their blocks.
- **[Archive & Results Controls](archive-controls.html)** — sort order, filter panels, and the Gutenberg block extensions.
- **[Search Overview](search-overview.html)** — how the search methods compare, and the results container and results pages that PowerSearch and the Search Widget share.
- **[PowerSearch](powersearch.html)** — the PowerSearch form, query syntax and block, and the native search override.
- **[Search Widget](search-widget.html)** — the advanced search widget, its fields and controllers.
- **[Editing Posts](editing-posts.html)** — the Tags field, restricting tags to existing ones, content-based tag suggestions, and the inclusion and container badges, redirect indicators, hidden terms and sticky pins in the post editor.
- **[Roles & Permissions](roles-permissions.html)** — the six custom capabilities and which roles get them.
- **[Export, Import & Migration](export-import-migration.html)** — JSON export/import and migrating from Tag Groups (now PublishPress Tag Groups).
- **[Deactivation & Uninstall](deactivation-uninstall.html)** — what stops working while the plugin is deactivated, what reactivation restores, and what uninstalling deletes.
- **[Modeling Guide](modeling-guide.html)** — how to structure content with HyperTaxonomy: patterns, design principles, and example scenarios.
- **[Working with Other Plugins](other-plugins.html)** — how HyperTaxonomy fits alongside ACF, Pods, TaxoPress, FacetWP, SEO and translation plugins, and others.
- **[FAQ & Troubleshooting](faq.html)** — common questions and troubleshooting.
