# HyperTaxonomy User Guide

For WordPress site owners and content editors who configure and use the plugin.

This guide is split into the following pages:

- **[Introduction & Concepts](introduction.html)** — what terms and taxonomies are, the problem HyperTaxonomy solves, relationship types, and Term Groups.
- **[Installation & Configuration](installation-configuration.html)** — requirements, installation, every settings tab, and the post-editor features.
- **[Term Relationships](term-relationships.html)** — Term Groups, inclusions, containers, synonyms, group inclusions, exclusions, and redirects.
- **[Per-Term Settings](per-term-settings.html)** — visibility, icons, archive descriptions, sticky posts, archive overrides, merge, and conversion.
- **[Search & PowerSearch](search.html)** — the advanced search widget, PowerSearch, native search override, and search shortcodes.
- **[Post List Shortcodes](post-list-shortcodes.html)** — `[ht_post_list]`, `[ht_related_posts]`, and their blocks.
- **[Display Shortcodes](display-shortcodes.html)** — `[ht_term_list]`, `[ht_term_index]`, `[ht_term_cloud]`, `[ht_term_groups]`, and their blocks.
- **[Archive & Results Controls](archive-controls.html)** — sort order, filter panels, and the Gutenberg block extensions.
- **[Import & Migration](import-migration.html)** — JSON export/import and migrating from Tag Groups (now PublishPress Tag Groups).
- **[FAQ & Deactivation](faq-deactivation.html)** — feature-choice guidance, FAQ, troubleshooting, deactivation, and uninstall.
- **[Best Practices & Modeling](best-practices.html)** — how to structure content with HyperTaxonomy and how it fits alongside other plugins.
